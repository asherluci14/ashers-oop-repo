Summary:
- Necromancer can summon and control undead creatures
- Necromancer gathers consumable magical resources for summoning rituals
- Every ritual requires ectoplasm
- Different undead require different combinations of:
    - Necrotic Runes
    - Spirit Runes
    - Bone Runes
    - Flesh Runes
- Summoned undead begin at level 1 with their own starting health + power
    - This can be levelled up



Classes:
- ResourceManager
- SummoningRitual
- Undead
- Necromancer

Justifications for potential deviations from instructions

- Necromancer has a compositional relationship with Undead:

        This is the code inside Necromancer.summon() where the Undead object is created:

        self.__undead.append(ritual.create_undead(self.__summon_id))

        However, Undead are technically instantiated in the SummoningRitual class. Despite this,
        Undead are not "owned" by the ritual, but rather are owned by the Necromancer. By that definition,
        I have classified a compositional relationship between Necromancer and Undead, because Undead
        is created inside Necromancer, it doesn't exist outside of Necromancer (it isn't just appended on),
        and the Necromancer owns the Undead.
