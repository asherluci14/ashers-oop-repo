Summary:
- Necromancer can summon and control undead creatures
- Necromancer gathers consumable magical resources for summoning rituals
- Every ritual requires ectoplasm
- Different undead require different combinations of:
    - Necrotic Runes
    - Spirit Runes
    - Bone Runes
    - Flesh Runes
- Summoned undead begin at level 1 with their own starting health + power
    - This can be levelled up
